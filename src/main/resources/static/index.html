Questions people ask

Who is Aal Sikke for?

Students, people who work, and people who do both. You choose Student, Work or Dual when you register, and you can change it later in your profile.

Do I have to verify my email?

Yes. After you register we send a verification link. Open it, then log in. If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder, or use the resend button on the login page.

How do I add a weekly class or shift?

Open Tasks and tap New task. Pick the category Class or Shift, tick “Has a start time” and “Repeat weekly”, then choose the days. It shows up on your calendar every week.

How do reminders work?

Tick “Remind me about this task” when you add it. You get a halfway reminder, and you can add your own. Reminders appear under the bell in the top bar, and you can switch on email reminders in Settings.

What does Conflict mean?

Two items overlap in time, for example a class and a shift. Both get an orange Conflict badge so you can move one before the clash happens.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open the site in your phone’s browser, nothing to install. The menu moves to the bottom of the screen on small devices.

Can I see my tasks in Google Calendar or my phone’s calendar?

Yes. On the Tasks page, choose Export to calendar. It downloads a file that Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Outlook can import.

I forgot my password. What now?

On the login page choose “Forgot password?” and enter your email. We send you a link to set a new one.

Is my data safe, and can I delete it?

The site uses HTTPS only. In Settings you can download a copy of your data or delete your account, which removes everything. Aal Sikke is still being tested with real users, so data may be reset if something goes badly wrong.
